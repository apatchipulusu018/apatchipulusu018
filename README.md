
# Welcome to my humble abode! ✧

I'm a senior Data Science student at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln's Jeffrey S. Raikes School of Computer Science and Management.

One of my favorite things is getting to learn something completely new and figuring out what I can do with it. I've always loved asking questions, understanding how things work, and finding different ways to approach a problem, which is what makes data science so exciting to me. There's something really interesting about taking a bunch of information and uncovering patterns or insights that weren't obvious before. But what I enjoy even more is figuring out what to do with those insights and how to turn them into something useful. Whether I'm working with AI, building an application, or exploring a new area of technology, I love the combination of problem-solving, creativity, and the opportunity to make a real difference.

### Things I've Built

**[BigByte](https://github.com/shaynajohn/BigByteOld)**

A group restaurant recommendation platform built around a problem we've all faced: trying to find somewhere to eat that makes everyone happy. We explored how individual preferences, constraints, and recommendation algorithms could come together to make group decisions a little easier.

**[Moodify](https://github.com/apatchipulusu018/Moodify_NLPRecommendationSystem)**

A music recommendation system that explores the relationship between language, emotion, and music. Using NLP, sentence embeddings, and clustering, I explored how someone could describe how they're feeling and discover music that matches their mood.
